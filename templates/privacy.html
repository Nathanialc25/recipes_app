What this app is

GoodEats is a personal recipe application. You can convert recipes from URLs, save them, and share them with friends. This page explains what data we handle and why.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture URL. That's it. We use this to identify you, display your name, and connect you with friends who share recipes.

Where it's stored

Your data lives in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Neon. It's encrypted in transit and at rest. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing the address below.

Third parties

The app uses Google for sign-in, Google Gemini for recipe parsing, and Neon for database hosting. Each of these services has its own privacy policy. No other third parties receive your data.
